## First review item · a refused retirement changes the draw

**Verified.** A single RETIRED call on either of two generated TEAM draws changes the stored result before v1 and v2 return `ERR_EXISTING_POSITION_ASSIGNMENT`. In the Laver Cup semifinal, the winner, score and timestamps change. In the feed-in consolation, the changes also reach the lineup and a side’s draw position. Differential rolls back and throws. The two cases each failed the unchanged-record assertion in all three modes on the handoff rerun. The ordinary baseline suite had passed 33,517 tests per mode. [Inputs, exact errors and reproduction command](findings/F-005.md)

**Recommended disposition.** Assign F-005 to v2 hardening independently of the v3 timetable. Trace the first unsafe placement in each input. Determine whether both fit the existing row 31 held-seat predicate or require an earlier placement check in the TEAM writer. The existing error-atomicity ruling already gives the acceptance test. In each standalone mode, both directives must return the expected refusal with the entire record unchanged. Differential must agree without throwing. Re-run the three full suites after a candidate fix.

## What to plan first

**Verified.** Of 9,397 successful whole-call recordings in the v2 suite, 3,972 were classed as exit, 2,314 as cascade and 968 as TEAM. Together they account for 77%. Coverage identified legacy functions reached for all 17 observed reasons, using sampled calls. Counts measure call frequency. They do not establish which functions can be deleted. The full report maps each reason to candidate ops, spec sections, risks and estimated effort. [Per-reason designs](REPORT.md#whole-call-census) · [counts](census.json) · [sampled writer reach](writer-reach.json)

**Verified.** The schedule prototype moved two calls into native planning. Its schedule count fell from five to three and every other bucket stayed constant. Differential caught an erroneous predicted notice in the first attempt. After that correction, v2 and differential each passed 33,512 tests with one failure. An existing assertion explicitly requires the old recording count. All 890 baseline manifest entries passed. Step-by-step harvested replay qualification remains open. [Prototype results](REPORT.md#schedule-prototype)

**Recommended sequence and decisions for CA**

1. **Schedule ownership.** Decide whether a native schedule-only write may replace a recording when its result, stored schedule, timestamps and ordered notices match. Recommend approving that contract as the criterion for this pilot. Any change to the ownership assertion requires CA’s ruling. Then require three green suites, a step-by-step replay and a census showing only the intended calls moved.
2. **Index experiment.** Exercise a planner that reads the retained index. Compare against fresh construction on state replacement, non-outcome writes and rollback as well as ordinary accepted batches. Require equivalent planner results and ordered notices, and measure end-to-end mutation time on the E2 workloads before estimating savings.
3. **Legacy writer retirement.** Design exit and cascade together around seat placement and withdrawal, then cover TEAM line/dual interaction. Retire a writer only after mapping remaining callers, accounting for every fallback, and proving parity on the fields CA retains. Keep the prior implementation available as a qualification oracle.
4. **8.x contract changes.** The selector removal and positional status-code sunset already have rulings. Seek separate decisions on notice and timestamp changes and on stored-record normalization. Recommend versioning observable notice changes after client/server acceptance. For a proposed migration, require idempotence and replay the same subsequent directives from original and migrated records. Declare each intentional hash change by rule. [Spec deltas and consumer effects](REPORT.md#spec-delta-and-landing-order)
