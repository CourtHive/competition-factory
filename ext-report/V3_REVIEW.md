## First review item · a refused retirement changes the draw

**Verified.** F-005 is one shared-writer defect on two TEAM retirements. V2’s row 31 preflight misses the held seat and hands the call to the v1 writer. The writer changes the record before returning `ERR_EXISTING_POSITION_ASSIGNMENT`. The Laver Cup semifinal retains a winner and score. The feed-in consolation also changes the lineup and a side’s draw position. Differential’s “v2 ok” describes the preflight decision. The independent unchanged-record assertion establishes the partial write. [Single-call fixtures, code path and repro](findings/F-005.md)

**Severity and action.** Schedule this as moderate v2 hardening. Direct engine callers and integrators without rollback can retain the changed record. TMX enables queue rollback locally and in the request to CFS. CFS passes the flag through and saves only successful results. With rollback enabled, both fixtures retained their original hashes and returned the correct error in both standalone modes. Extend the shared pre-write check behind row 31, then verify both fixtures return the refusal with an identical record under v1 and v2 with rollback disabled. Differential should agree and the three ordinary suites should pass. [Consumer call sites and acceptance criteria](findings/F-005.md)

## What to plan first

**Verified.** Of 9,397 successful whole-call recordings in the v2 suite, 3,972 were classed as exit, 2,314 as cascade and 968 as TEAM. Together they account for 77%. Coverage identified legacy functions reached for all 17 observed reasons, using sampled calls. Counts measure call frequency. They do not establish which functions can be deleted. The full report maps each reason to candidate ops, spec sections, risks and estimated effort. [Per-reason designs](REPORT.md#whole-call-census) · [counts](census.json) · [sampled writer reach](writer-reach.json)

**Verified.** The schedule prototype moved two calls into native planning. Its schedule count fell from five to three and every other bucket stayed constant. Differential caught an erroneous predicted notice in the first attempt. After that correction, v2 and differential each passed 33,512 tests with one failure. An existing assertion explicitly requires the old recording count. All 890 baseline manifest entries passed. Step-by-step harvested replay qualification remains open. [Prototype results](REPORT.md#schedule-prototype)

**Recommended sequence and decisions for CA**

1. **Schedule ownership.** Decide whether a native schedule-only write may replace a recording when its result, stored schedule, timestamps and ordered notices match. Recommend approving that contract as the criterion for this pilot. Any change to the ownership assertion requires CA’s ruling. Then require three green suites, a step-by-step replay and a census showing only the intended calls moved.
2. **Index experiment.** Exercise a planner that reads the retained index. Compare against fresh construction on state replacement, non-outcome writes and rollback as well as ordinary accepted batches. Require equivalent planner results and ordered notices, and measure end-to-end mutation time on the E2 workloads before estimating savings.
3. **Legacy writer retirement.** Design exit and cascade together around seat placement and withdrawal, then cover TEAM line/dual interaction. Retire a writer only after mapping remaining callers, accounting for every fallback, and proving parity on the fields CA retains. Keep the prior implementation available as a qualification oracle.
4. **8.x contract changes.** The selector removal and positional status-code sunset already have rulings. Seek separate decisions on notice and timestamp changes and on stored-record normalization. Recommend versioning observable notice changes after client/server acceptance. For a proposed migration, require idempotence and replay the same subsequent directives from original and migrated records. Declare each intentional hash change by rule. [Spec deltas and consumer effects](REPORT.md#spec-delta-and-landing-order)

5. **Fallback cost (OUTCOME-101 / G4).** Recommend keeping the per-population cost obligation open for CA. The 11 November ruling conditioned the default switch on separating admission from replay or moving the affected calls into native ownership. The later flip criterion covered soak and walk parity. The reviewed rulings contain no explicit disposition of the earlier cost condition. The close-out still measured whole-call recording at 1.5–2.6× v1 on its sampled calls. Record the 7.44.0 switch as shipped and seek an explicit disposition. Before selector removal, map the old populations to current calls, rerun the applicable G4 bounds with paired v1 controls, and bring each remaining excess to CA for remediation or explicit acceptance. E2 and E4 answer different performance questions. ## Qualification limits for the reviewer

**Verified.** Public deterministic replay matched the recorded result codes and step hashes for 2,855 tournament scenarios and 67,918 steps across all three modes. The harvest is diagnostic evidence. Of 41,829 harvested scenarios, four mode-disagreement rows reduce to two F-005 inputs. Standalone v1 and v2 produced identical partial writes. Differential exposed the admission disagreement, while the unchanged-record assertion showed the contract breach. Agreement between modes alone cannot detect a shared writer error.

**Triage.** All three modes departed from the harvested expectation on 949 scenarios (2.27%). Fifteen already differed on load, three threw, and 202 returned a different directive result. In 729 the normalized results matched but a state hash differed, including 31 whose replay never left its initial state. In the head-to-head example the original test adds each generated draw separately. All eight additions are absent from the replay directives. A mocks example similarly requests a score on a draw whose creation is absent. The remaining signatures need case-level attribution. Use independent refusal and draw invariants alongside replay, and keep the harvest qualification gate open. [Triage method and examples](REPORT.md#harvest-drift-triage) · [all classified scenarios](harvest-drift-triage.json)

The harvest run had 68 recording-mode failures, and the official coverage reader exceeded its string-size limit. The private fixed corpus, production soak and private TEAM fuzz harness were unavailable to this study. [Baseline and replay evidence](REPORT.md#2-baseline)
